Nethack

NetHack DevTeam  ·  1987

2025-12  ·  2026  · 

2025-12  ·  roguelike  ·  game   video-game


Nethack is an open source single-player roguelike video game, first released in 1987 and maintained by the NetHack DevTeam. The game is originally a fork of the 1984 game Hack, itself inspired by the 1980 game Rogue, and is one of the longest continually developed games out there, making it (despite its lo-fi graphics) one of the most complex games out there.

Here follows my early account of playing:

(29/12/2025) Today was my first day playing XNetHack, and NetHack at large. While initially disappointed that I only had a tile-set version working, doubly so that I could not get the orientation update working so I could be a gay Valkyria, thus the whole personal point of using the XNetHack fork (we all have to strive for something, am I right?). I did have a fun experience in a similar way to Brogues’ fun in wandering briefly before impending death. I was mostly preoccupied with assuring Fimus, my pet cat, whom I spawned with and named, was in good shape. So much so that I followed them into the trap door they triggered when we were roaming the dungeon in a fit of panic (they were fine). We murdered our way through many a newt and kobold. At least until I read a mysterious scroll that turned me into a were-rat. A position that was not remedied by the slew of items I was carrying.

A were status is like a hiccup, where almost randomly, each move turn, I had a chance of returning to rat. Long story short, this eventually led me naked next to an affronted hobbit who ended my plight as level 4 vegan Valkeryia Alixi. (I did later learn that I could have prayed to a number of deities who likely would have fixed my status - side note, this is also useful for removing cursed armour!)

Day two 30/12/2025: (installed it on my PhD desktop, adjusting the config before running it) After orientation config file update: Yays seems the gay orientation config is working : )

Tried a new game and immediately ran into two succubi, one who became enthralled with me and proceeded to follow and engage (love me up) with me 4 more times. This, on top of the initial two introductory fornications, at which time my character died from over exhaustion.

This game rocks, but 9/10 for unrealistic sapphic sex portrayal. Should’ve been able to survive at least 4 additional times… 🙃

Thus ended my initial gameplay log, as did my characters’ le petit mort prior to their big one. But I’ll be back to keep trying to get to and retrieve the sacred Amulet of Yonder, and meanwhile, work towards improving my stats for more succubus trysts.

(While it’s always hard to judge when you have “played” or “experienced” a game, I usually identify if I have beat/reached the end of the game or contributed multiple hours to feel confident in identifying progression, narrative, and mechanical relationships.)
