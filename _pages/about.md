Kia ora & Salutations! Welcome to my website.

I am Alix, a nerdy artist who explores quirky game development, composes wonky lyrical indie rock, and pontificates on various geekery via adventuring through possessed hyper-focused research and practical experiments. Currently, I am incredibly ecstatic to be a PhD in Design candidate at Victoria University of Wellington in New Zealand.

Come join me and stay awhile as I post about my exciting expeditions and misadventures of building nifty games, writing queer tunes, crafting codeful tutorials, and share various interesting tidbits and ditties I come across during my erudite excursions.

Here is where I write on my thoughts of where I have gone, plan to go -and why it is cool for my studies and for your contemplation. As a deviant diviner of kluge, a writrix slinging smithed ink in digital famtldahyde, an invocator of ludonarrative systems ever added to the never-ending soup and songs of our world... and as an general epistemophilic, you may find a wide spread of interests and connections.
But for the sake of theme, and past me hoping to guide and direct my PhD investigations/wanderings, the core explorations are grounded currently in human-authored procedural generation, queer representation in video games, and lo-fi glyph repurposing as graphics in game genres like traditional roguelikes. Outside of that, we will occasionally misadventure to the wilds and weeds of historicity of technology and a few quirky game development topics at large.
