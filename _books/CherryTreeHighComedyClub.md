Created by a prodominatly one man team 773 handle “Hizume” CTHCC is quite a fun stats manager platonic dating sim. what makes it is the exceptional writing humor, charmign graphics, and spunky music. Its akin to a combatless Persona game, and with my recent inspiration from PrincessRL of time management with stats improvment to recuit peers to your comedy club you are trying to start. There is that usual silly event progression subspace warping, you want to travel around town three time, no time passes but playing inthe arcade or watching a film in the theatre, thats your whole afternoon. (look at atlas book on persona 5).

Major bonus for the heavy shappic/yuri hints.
