In the process of reading and taking notes for this work.

Interesting first chapter, it presented a nice ending rhythm showing the layers of complexity of not just the historicity, but also how we can understand sexuality culturally and personally, but identifying the steeped challenges that permeate and perpetuate the gaps in understanding, equality, and tolerance. I am about 1/3 of the way through.

The over all theme is there are no binaries just a need for more education, and our long history of what was thought to be good practice and classification and foudned in sexual medical practice is really fueled and shaped by a heteronormative patriarchal capitalism colonism etc., which is still being shed for deeper understandings of normal/abnormal, functionality, majorities/minorities.
