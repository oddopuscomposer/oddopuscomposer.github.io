It was quite challenging to procure this book (shout out to my friend Raki, who was kind enough to send me the copy I had to order to their house in Germany!) But I am ecstatic to get a chance to explore a personal fascination of mine with the historicity of LGBTQ representation in video games. The book documents Adrienne Shaw's (queer game studies extraordinaire) museum show from 2019, as well as containing some insight into her research.
