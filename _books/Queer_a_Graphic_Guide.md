Part of the trilogy of Graphic Guides (queer, gender, & sexuality), I had been meaning to start some fun queer theory foundation.

I am compiling my notes, while a lot of good concepts and a well-rounded survey of subjects, my main critique is that the author lacks providing direct references, and when I conduct further research, their claims often seem quantitatively skewed, which is frustrating because generally I agree with almost everything said, but am frustrated at the lack of academic rigor in their referencing.
