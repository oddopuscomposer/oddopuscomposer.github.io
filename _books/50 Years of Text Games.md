This encyclopaedic over 600-page tome by Aaron A. Reed is a love letter to Text Adventure Games, featuring in-depth consideration for a noted game released each year for the last half century. When I saw many particular favorites (Violet, Dwarf Fortress, Fallen London, 80 Days - though it did miss out on two ones I will note here, 9:05 by Adam Cadre from 2000, and The Wizard Sniffer by Buster Hudson from 2017) listed, I knew it was a must-have.

I teach making text adventures in my Evolution of Video Game Design & Culture course and so have been steeped in Inform 7 IDE for a few years now, enjoying the linguistics of parser UIs and pedantry of textual worlds.

Anyway, exceptionally excited to read through, though potentially only time I might have to dedicate currently may be reading more around 10 years out of the 50 offered.
