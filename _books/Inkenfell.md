I started playing, and I have to get back to it, Ikenfell. During the hour I played, it seemed like a refreshing twist on tactic RPG design with a Paper or Mario RPG-style timed battle system. It improves the combat formula a bit by incorporating a spatial strategy element. This, in turn, provides a small situational grid to navigate your characters and to similarly plan your moves, which can be measured and distributed in grid-highlighted patterns. The quick-timed events when in battle are challenging in a good way, threading a space of keeping you interested and active during battles without regular frustration. Additionally, I thought the writing was a standout element of the game with intriguing characters and modern sensibilities, which meshed well with charming minimalist pixel art. Hopefully, I'll get back to it and finish it soon!
