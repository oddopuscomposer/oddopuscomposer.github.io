Trans*: A Quick and Quirky Account of Gender Variability
 I think this work was refreshingly easy to read, being the perfect mix of entertaining and topical with proper references. It really is situated in a kind mix of relevance and interest for me. That being said, I can already see some optimism that feels dated, being my version is from 2018. I wonder what Jack Haberstam thinks now about some things with much more dismal universal realities of trans oppression, scapegoating, and hate violence being lobbied, excused, and encouraged in 2026. I look forward to and will be reading their next release coming in November (2026), as it seems similarly enchantingly placed as my intersectional interests of the constructed body for my studies - Architecture After Everything: A Trans Manifesto.
