I am quite enjoying playing this long tactical RPG. It has some exceptionally creative (at least to me as a first-time Fire Emblem experience) mechanics around improving relationships between your student (soldiers). There are excellent nuance to improving and switching characters class (kind of like a job system) and their equipment. Whats more is the story is advanced in little animated vignettes depending on how often they are engaging and supporting each other on the battlefield. There are also delightful quiet times allowing you to explore the large campus happening (a romantic academic as myself certainly adores this) where you can continue to strive to improve character relations, stats, and get little personality quips from the NPCs at large. I enjoy the calendar system as well, breaking down the narrative over a long, marked period which is also on theme for when you (the young mercenary hotshot professor) instruct them and actually give them time to reasonably become improved in their skills.

Major plus for multiple shappic paths for Lady Byleth and an impressive story around questioning the established systems of religion and class. Admittedly this inspires a game design doc of a college adventure that, hopefully, I'll get a chance to start implementing someday.
