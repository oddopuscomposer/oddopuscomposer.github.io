Dungeon Boyfriend is a fun roguelike dating simulator where you, as a plucky new arrival, start delving dungeon in a modern, if urban fantasy, Verona Beach, California USA. You start to make new friends, despite your cousin's awful attempts to assist, and begin to date around the balmy beach city. Soon, you find out there is an old mall, which is a procedural roguelike hub of contemporary enemies made up of animated record players, personified cellphones, and other inanimate animated beasts.
The major unique twist? Well, you know your weapon of choice? Well its someone you can date, you see in this world some people can transform into a weapon (anime fans rejoice), so your relationship may progresses in a multitude of pathways from just bonding through mall dungeon crawling together, to special story events around town much in the style of a traditional kinetic novel, to the classic give a gift (and prior crafting of said gift) often known so well from Stardew Valley and its precursor the Harvest Moon games. This bonding also has mechanical implementations, such as increasing power and moves with said weapon peeps and also is reinforced through the overarching narrative as you are a welder of weapon(s), (hey its a job to clear out an old mall), and you and all your new friends are also trying to find out who has been capturing and harming weapon people. Perhaps, just maybe, you'll find love along the way too <3

This game was great at introducing new elements and people often throughout the game to keep things fresh, from having other weapons to delve with to giving you opportunities to hone in and focus on the relationship simulator aspect of building up your connection with one or multiple eligible bachelorxs.

The roguelike aspect was a little straightforward, but with a good challenge curve, and the sentient weapons keep things interesting with increasing power and optional moves for those who wish to attempt to make it feel a little less hack n' slash. Procedural generation was sensical, with the occasional fun secret or challenge that helped the player suffering from the compulsive gumption feel rewarded in their required clearing of the whole flow before riding the escalator deeper into the maw of the mall.

Dating-wise, I really appreciate for a game named Boyfriend Dungeon, they have a variety of "dating" options. Though dating is not quite the correct term because while there is a refreshing variety of identities and orientations, there is also a more friendship style al la Splunky platonics, where you can just hang out with a cat... who can also turn into a weapon. Kitfox Games said they wanted to make the game "as inclusive as possible," which feels quite earnest, as even with the player character, you are able to select from a multitude of pronouns, or even if you are polyamorous.

All in all, great game, love the genre mashing of roguelike and relationship simulator, and the relatively quick pace of events to keep things from feeling stagnant, while the roguelike play adds a much-appreciated element (at least for me) of player agency and skill to dating sims. It feels like a kind way to introduce one genre's fans to another's mechanics, but also something refreshing for the general lover of video games, with the added bonus of a good understanding of relationships, identity, and orientation, which helped it feel like a good attempt at providing options for everyone with decent representation.
