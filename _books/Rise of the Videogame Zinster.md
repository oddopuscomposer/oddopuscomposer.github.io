Anna Anthropy's *Rise of th Videogame Zinseter: How Freaks, Normals, Amateurs, Artists, Dreamers, Dropouts, Queers, Housewives, and People, Like You are Taking Back an Art Form*, is an inspirational read that gets you in the mood to be radically creative and iterative.

Her book is a bit dated, as it was just as the crest of diy indie game development was becoming exceptionally available through tools and through success. While we had moments of Cave Story, Spelunky, and Braid, it would still be a few years until many others would join, from Thomas Was Alone, Stardew Valley, and Celeste, to name a few.

What I liked by her writing was the merging of autobiographic aspects of her own developmental journey and positionality and the book encourages a lot of tinkering and some basics on tools to play around with, which probably in 2012 I would have been quite interested in my early dabbling of RPG maker and Unity, but now have a decade of tinkering and the available engines and my personal game development interest expanding the DIY portions were mostly interesting from a historic of what online distribution excitement was permeating at the time.
