Titanium Court is delightful in a seemingly non-sequitur way. It oozes style, which is an easy starting place when talking about this game. It has beautiful, restrictive palettes focusing on blues and pink with a touch of green and pale mustard. But there are many great things to talk about with this game; it just gets a bit befuddled in the wonkiness of existing faedom - in the best possible way.

At first blush, you feel like you stumbled onto a peculiarly stylistic match-three game with some humorous writing capturing the strangeness of fae and tricksters in a pseudo-modern era, but then it expands into delightful genre-mashing, adding a tower defense aspect to each match-three adjustment, which effectively allows you to adjust the battlefield. Very creative, and despite the quirky, surreal narrative, it thematically is tied quite well together. Also, major points for a female lead!

I am still compiling my notes and thoughts, but I have to say I love the theme, as it is fun and silly while strung into a story. The characterisation of your court members is similarly successful and interesting. I am finding myself drawn quite evocatively to the strangeness of a fae world's interpretation, and the excellently loose and weird indie western(?) rock instrumentals.
