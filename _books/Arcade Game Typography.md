Overall an enjoyable romp through 8x8 hyperfixation of fonts in old arcade games that go surprisingly up into the first few years of the 2000s for those developments still lingering with minimalistic pixel fonts. I learned some interesting lore and graphic design tidbits and even developed a quirky list of 10 wonky games to try out that I was unaware of. The monospaced and magnetic tape section was one of my favorite insights from historical and technical perspectives (19in CRTs etc.), and His phrasing throughout describing some fonts as "both awesome and awful" is the kind of hyperfocused nich nerdery I can appreciate. The book also kept things fresh with his little brief comments on gameplay or translation -
