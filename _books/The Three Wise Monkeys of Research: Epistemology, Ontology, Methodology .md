I felt like I needed a brief foundation of methodology and general PhD practice, and who better than Tara Brabazon? She writes considerably on the subject and delightfully so has many of her resources free, so I picked up a couple of audio readings of her works (read by her) talking on some of the researcher mindset, academic taxonomies, and general PhD considerations.
